Airline Manager 4 — Detailed Description

🔎 Open Hub Planner

What This Tool Does

You pick your hub and enter your available capital. The tool checks every destination from that hub that has demand in the game (around 3,900 airports) against all 311 passenger aircraft available in the AM4 order menu. It shows which routes, flown with which aircraft type, earn the most profit in the selected period and are affordable with your capital.

For a single route including depreciation and resale, use the Route Calculator.

Hub & Capital

  • Country / Airport — Pick the country first (all countries in the game, alphabetical), then your hub airport (with IATA code). The hub's runway length is shown below the selection.
  • Available capital ($) — The amount you want to spend on aircraft. Only aircraft whose purchase price fits your capital are considered.
  • Base — If your hub is a base with an upgraded runway, tick this box. The hub's runway is then ignored and only the destination runway limits the aircraft choice. In Easy mode the box is greyed out, because runway length does not matter there.

Capital Use

Single Aircraft

Finds the best combination of route and aircraft type whose purchase price fits your capital. The result is shown as a green card.

Multiple, one per route

Spreads the capital across up to “Max. Number of Aircraft”, each on its own route. The tool looks for the combination with the highest total profit, respecting capital and aircraft count at the same time. Any unused amount is shown as remaining capital.

Multiple, several per route

As above, but a high-demand route can get several aircraft of the same type. They share the route's demand equally. Another aircraft is only added if it increases the route's profit. This mode never yields less profit than “one per route”.

Both multiple modes show a purple card with the fleet plan: destination, number and type, configuration, departures per day, revenue, costs, profit and investment per route, plus a total row.

Costs & Settings

  • Fuel — $/1000 lbs (default = 500)
  • CO₂ Quota — $/1000 units, as shown in the game (default = 140)
  • Period — Days for which revenue, costs and profit are calculated (default = 20)
  • Flight Hours / Day — Per aircraft (default = 20)
  • Pilots, Crew, Engineers, Technicians — $/day (default = 200, 150, 250, 225). Salaries can be found in the game under "Airline" → "Staff"; they change with pay raises and cuts.
  • Max. Departures / Day per Aircraft — As many departures as you realistically start per day (default = 10)
  • Max. Number of Aircraft — Fleet limit for the multiple modes (default = 3); greyed out for Single Aircraft
  • Game Mode — Easy or Realism (default = Realism)
  • Capital Use — See above

Results

Route List

Below the card, all profitable routes from the hub are listed with their best aircraft type: destination, distance, runway, demand Y J F, configuration, departures per day, revenue, costs and profit for the period, purchase price and payback (after how many days the profit covers the purchase price). The list can be sorted by any column, searched by destination or aircraft, and shows 100 routes per page.

Click a row to open the cost breakdown (staff, maintenance, fuel, CO₂) and up to six alternative aircraft types for that route.

Calculation

Departures per Day

The flight time of a departure is the one-way trip: Flight Time = Distance / Speed. In Easy mode, aircraft fly 1.5× faster. Departures per day are Flight Hours per Day / Flight Time (rounded down), capped by “Max. Departures / Day”. If the flight time exceeds the flight hours per day, it is calculated proportionally (e.g. 0.8 departures per day).

Revenue

Ticket prices follow the game formulas (Easy/Realism) with optimal multipliers (Y ×1.1 J ×1.08 F ×1.06). As in the game, each price is rounded down to whole dollars and reduced by $2. The seat configuration is optimized by F>J>Y priority based on demand; free seats are counted as economy. Revenue is only earned for passengers with demand.

Costs

  • Staff — Daily salaries × crew size of the aircraft type
  • Maintenance — A-check cost per flight: A-check price × flight hours (rounded up to whole hours) / maintenance interval, doubled in Realism mode; plus wear of 0.00075% of the purchase price per flight
  • Fuel — Distance × consumption (lbs/km) × price / 1000
  • CO₂ Quota — (Distance × CO₂ coefficient × passengers + seats) × price / 1000; passengers weighted Y ×1, J ×2, F ×3

Depreciation and resale are not part of the Hub Planner. Profit is revenue minus these four cost types.

Runways and Range

  • Realism — The shorter runway of hub and destination counts; with “Base” only the destination runway
  • Easy — Runway length does not matter
  • Stopovers are not considered: if a destination is beyond an aircraft's range, that type is excluded for the route. Destinations under 100 km are not calculated.

Model Limits

The results are estimates. They assume you use every aircraft without gaps within the set flight hours and departures and fully use the demand. Competition on the route, reputation, fuel and CO₂ price changes and in-game training are not included.
